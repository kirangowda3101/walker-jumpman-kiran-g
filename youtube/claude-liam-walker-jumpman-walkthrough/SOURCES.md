## Footage

All gameplay is real engine output from this session — five scripted-input
captures at native 3840×2160, listed with SHA-256 hashes in `coverage.json` and
documented in `CAPTURE.md`. Nothing is re-created, re-drawn, re-tinted or
animated. No screenshots, no stock footage, no generated imagery.

The game contains no audio of any kind, so nothing was muted and no sound
effects were invented.

## Visuals that are not gameplay

Five Remotion scenes from the brutalist.art library, rendered at 3840×2160:
`ClaudeComposerAsk` (B00, BHTF), `BrutalistHesitantWriter` (B01),
`ClaudeVerdictArtifact` (BVDT), `ClaudeTitleOutro` (BOUT). Deterministic —
B01's seed is `walker-jumpman-fork-2026`, and the outro's mascot and polarity
are seeded by the reel slug per `OUTRO-LOCK.md`.

## Human and AI contributions to the film

Consistent with the split the project's own `SOURCES.md` records for the game.

**AI (Claude, via Claude Code) produced:** the capture driver; the jump-mark
search for the ground route; the beat sheet, narration script and all reel
documents; the evidence mapping in `coverage.json`; the frame inspections and
the fact-check.

**Human decisions in this session:** the reel's location, the episode title
("The Fork That Wasn't."), the decision to keep the progress-bar defect in the
verdict, and one substantive script correction — an earlier draft of B06 said
"four pixels of headroom", conflating the 32 px clearance with the 4 px margin
over a 28 px collider. That line was rewritten before any audio was generated.
The full script was reviewed and approved before narration was produced.

**Not claimed:** no human has playtested the game for this film. The captures
are scripted input. The only human play evidence for this project remains the
author's own, in `TEST-REPORT.md` §5, and the absence of an external
playtester is stated out loud in the verdict.

## Corrections applied during authoring

Logged per DOUBLE-CHECK LAW; full detail in `FACTCHECK.md` §3–4.

1. "four pixels of headroom" → "thirty-two pixels of headroom … four to spare."
2. A ceiling-bump claim was cut after arithmetic and a frame inspection showed
   the player passes through a platform gap and touches nothing.
3. Route times are this session's scripted-input results (11.3 s / 12.3 s),
   labelled as such — not the author's human times or the solver's optima.
4. `CHANGE-BRIEF.md` says the level has ten solids; `first_steps.json` has 12.
   The film uses 12.
5. The HUD progress-bar defect at `hud.gd:24` is not recorded in any of the
   project's documents. It was found while reading `hud.gd` and confirmed on
   screen before being put in the verdict.
